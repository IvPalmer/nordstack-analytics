NordStack billed €14,003 of MRR in July 2026 across 107 paying subscriptions, after peaking at €15,484 in December 2025. The scale plan carries 65% of billed revenue. Since January 2024, 46 subscriptions have churned, taking €7,114 of contractual monthly value with them; 24 of the 46 cancellations fall in the last twelve months.

The 2,855 invoices were classified row by row and 9 were quarantined with a stated reason. Tests reconcile paid amounts, customer revenue and churn totals back to the raw export, to the cent.

Definitions. MRR is billed: the sum of paid invoice amounts in the invoice month, converted to EUR. Lost MRR on churn is contractual: the monthly price of subscriptions whose cancellation date falls in the month, on or before the reporting cutoff. Customer lifetime value is paid revenue attributable to a customer record. The two revenue bases differ on purpose and are labelled where they appear.

Reproduce. make bootstrap builds the warehouse from a clean clone: MySQL as the source system, dlt to Postgres, dbt models and tests (source diagnostics warn, everything downstream errors). An Airflow DAG reruns the chain every five minutes when deployed; the report is a separate make report.
